## Timeline

### 2021-01-10 — Announcement (baseline promise) **[VERIFIER: CORRECTED — two announcements were conflated]**
- **10 Jan 2021:** MBS announces THE LINE: 170 km long, **1 million residents**, zero cars / zero streets / zero carbon emissions, 100% renewable, end-to-end in 20 minutes, construction to start Q1 2021, cost put at US$100–200 bn. **No height, no mirror, no "9 million" yet.** **[OFFICIAL]**
  Source: NEOM newsroom / SPA, 2021-01-10 — https://www.neom.com/en-us/newsroom/hrh-announces-the-line ; Reuters 2021-01-10.
- **25 Jul 2022:** the design reveal added the specs the video will show: **500 m tall, 200 m wide, mirrored exterior facades, 9 million residents**, 34 km² footprint, "vertical layering"; the WSJ had already reported the internal name **"Mirror Line"** (미러 라인) on 24 Jul 2022. **[OFFICIAL]**
  Sources: NEOM newsroom 2022-07-25 — https://www.neom.com/en-us/newsroom/hrh-crown-prince-announces-the-designs-of-the-line ; Dezeen 2022-07-26 — https://www.dezeen.com/2022/07/26/the-line-neom-saudi-arabia-mirrored-skyscraper/ ; WSJ 2022-07-24 "Saudi Arabia's plan for a mirror-clad skyscraper city".
- The combined spec set (170 km / 500 m / 9 M / car-free / mirrored) was then restated in every autumn-2022 construction report, e.g. The National 2022-10-20 — https://www.thenationalnews.com/gulf-news/saudi-arabia/2022/10/20/drone-reveals-saudi-arabias-the-line-under-construction/
  > Video implication: the "promise" title card should say **2021 발표 → 2022 디자인 공개** rather than dating the 500 m mirror to January 2021.
