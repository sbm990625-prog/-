Compiled 2026-09-27 for the video "사우디의 네온 시티, 진짜로 지어졌다면?" (what if THE LINE had been built as promised).

## 2. THE LINE announcement — 10 Jan 2021 (televised video address by MBS)

**Headline quote (the "launch film" line):**
> "Today, I present to you The Line, a city of a million residents with a length of 170 km that preserves 95 percent of nature within NEOM, with zero cars, zero streets and zero carbon emissions."
— [NEOM press release via PR Newswire, 2021-01-10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hrh-prince-mohammed-bin-salman-announces-the-line-at-neom-301204772.html)

**Other lines from the same address:**
> "Why should we sacrifice nature for the sake of development?"
> "We need to transform the concept of a conventional city into that of a futuristic one."
— [Arab News, 2021-01-10](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1790351/saudi-arabia)

**Promised specs (Jan 2021 version):**
- 170 km "belt of hyper-connected future communities, without cars and roads and built around nature"
- "first time in 150 years that a major urban development has been designed around people, not roads"; **all essential daily services within a five-minute walk**
- "ultra-high-speed transit and autonomous mobility"; **no journey longer than 20 minutes**
- **1 million residents**; **380,000 jobs**; **SAR 180 bn (~$48 bn) added to GDP by 2030**; construction to start Q1 2021
- powered exclusively by renewable energy; AI-driven ("cognitive city")
Sources: PR Newswire release above; [Argaam, 2021-01-10](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1434648); [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/saudi-arabia-the-line-neom-megacity).
Korean coverage: 이데일리 "사우디 스마트시티 '첫 단추'…'車 없는 탄소배출 제로 도시'" (2021-01-11) — [link](https://www.edaily.co.kr/news/read?newsId=02886406628916736&mediaCodeNo=257); 이투데이 "車 없는 도시 추진" — [link](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/1983002).

## 3. THE LINE design reveal — 25 Jul 2022 (mirror façade)

**MBS quote:**
> "At the launch of THE LINE last year, we committed to a civilizational revolution that puts humans first, providing an unprecedented urban living experience while preserving the surrounding nature."
— [Saudi Press Agency, 2022-07-25](https://www.spa.gov.sa/2372273); [NEOM newsroom](https://www.neom.com/en-us/newsroom/hrh-announces-theline-designs); [AAP copy of release](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20220725ae24033/)

**Official specs, verbatim wording from the release:**
- "THE LINE will eventually accommodate **9 million residents** and will be built on a footprint of just **34 square kilometers**"
- "only **200 meters wide**, but **170 kilometers long** and **500 meters above sea level**" (note: official text says *above sea level*, widely reported as 500 m tall)
- "an environment free from **roads, cars and emissions**"; "run on **100% renewable energy**"; "**95% of land preserved for nature**"
- "access to all facilities within a **five-minute walk**, in addition to a **high-speed rail with an end-to-end transit of 20 minutes**"
- "an **ideal climate all-year-round**"
- **Mirror façade:** "THE LINE will have an **outer mirror façade** that will provide its unique character and allow even its small footprint to blend with nature, while the interior will be built to create extraordinary experiences and magical moments." — [CNN, 2022-07](https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/saudi-arabia-the-line-city-scli-intl); [NPR, 2022-07-26](https://www.npr.org/2022/07/26/1113670047/saudi-arabia-new-city-the-mirror-line-desert)
- **Zero Gravity Urbanism:** "built around the idea of **layering city functions vertically** while giving people the possibility of moving seamlessly in **three dimensions (up, down or across)**" — [designboom, 2022-07-26](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/saudi-arabia-designs-the-line-zero-gravity-vertical-city-no-cars-07-26-2022/)
- **Microclimate:** "To ensure the establishment of microclimatic spaces, the environment has been carefully designed to allow for an **optimal balance of sunlight, shade and natural ventilation**." — release; [Archilovers project page](https://www.archilovers.com/projects/304348/the-line.html)

**Tarek Qaddumi (NEOM Executive Director, Urban Planning / The Line design), Aug 2022:**
> "Zero Gravity Urbanism is an expression we use to describe the reduced dependence on the natural ground plane by creating multiple vertically organized ground planes at height." — [World-Architects](https://world-architects.com/en/architecture-news/headlines/designs-for-the-line-exhibited-in-venice)
> "Irrespective of its physical stature, it will end up demonstrating that the world has the human capacity, the technology and the commitment to revolutionise our current way of life." — [Dezeen, 2022-08-11](https://www.dezeen.com/2022/08/11/the-line-megacity-interview-tarek-qaddumi/)
Dezeen paraphrase: the linear + 3-D form "will allow for the creation of a microclimate that responds to the prevailing weather conditions"; The Line "will be self-shading, allowing optimal amounts of light, as well as natural ventilation, enabling the constant cooling of the public realm."

**WSJ "Mirror Line" leak (same week, 25 Jul 2022):** confidential planning documents showed two reflective buildings up to **488 m (1,600 ft)** high running parallel for **120 km (75 mi)**; cost **~$1 trillion**; ~**5 million** people; end-to-end in **20 minutes**; a Jan 2021 impact assessment said it "would have to be constructed in stages and could take **50 years** to complete." — [Fortune, 2022-07-25](https://fortune.com/2022/07/25/saudi-arabia-75-mile-long-skyscrapers-neom/); [Al Jazeera, 2022-07-25](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/7/25/saudi-arabia-to-build-1tr-mirrored-skyscraper-in-neom); [The National, 2022-07-25](https://www.thenationalnews.com/gulf-news/2022/07/25/mirror-line-saudi-arabias-neom-city-state-to-build-the-worlds-longest-skyscraper/).

## 7. Early expert pushback on the vision (for balance)

- Dezeen (2022-08-08): sustainability and liveability claims called "naive" by experts. — [link](https://www.dezeen.com/2022/08/08/sustainability-liveability-the-line-saudi-170km-city-naive/)

- Guardian (2022-07-27, Oliver Wainwright): "habitable supercomputer", "ominous dystopian undertone". — [Archinect summary](https://archinect.com/news/article/150323438/oliver-wainwright-explores-the-architectural-history-behind-saudi-arabia-s-planned-the-line-megacity)

Eng. & Arch., 2025 — questions whether natural ventilation can hold the microclimate as temperatures rise. — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43995-025-00103-6)

## Open questions / caveats
1. All page fetches were blocked by the sandbox proxy; figures come from search snippets of the cited pages. Re-verify exact wording (esp. the MBS quotes) against the SPA/NEOM releases before rendering as on-screen text.
2. Official 2022 text says "500 meters above sea level", not "500 m tall"; media universally report 500 m height. Decide which to show.
3. The $1.6 tn (2021) / $4.5 tn (2022) internal figures are FT-sourced via secondary reports — label as "reported internal estimates".
4. Exact narration of the 2017 NEOM launch film and the 2022 THE LINE film could not be retrieved; the MBS Jan-2021 video-address lines above are the best verifiable "launch film" quotes.
5. "9 million by 2045" comes from a NEOM/Tonomus page, not a dated press release.
