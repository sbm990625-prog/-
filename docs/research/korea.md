**Method & caveat.** This sweep ran 3 WebSearch queries before the session's shared search budget (200/200) was exhausted, and every WebFetch/curl to a news, wiki, portal or video host was refused by the network egress policy (HTTP 403 CONNECT). Facts below therefore come from (a) the search-engine extracts of the three completed searches and (b) Korean-language items already recorded, with URLs and dates, by the sibling sweeps in this folder (`reality.md`, `vision.md`, `critique.md`, `whatif.md`), each of which hit the same block. ## 1. 용어 — how Korean media name the thing

### K1. "네옴시티" (붙여쓰기) + "더 라인" is the dominant pairing — confidence: high
Every 2026 Korean headline on the tunnel-contract termination uses 네옴시티 as one word and 더 라인/더라인 for the linear city: 아시아경제 2026-03-13 "삼성물산·현대건설, 사우디 네옴시티 터널공사 계약 해지"; 경향신문 2026-03-13 "…'사우디 네옴시티 터널공사' 계약 해지"; 머니투데이 2026-03-17 "'제2의 중동붐 멈추나'…삼성물산·현대건설 네옴시티 공사 계약 해지". ### K2. "네온 시티" is not a media term; it is a mishearing of 네옴(NEOM) — confidence: medium
NEOM = *neo* (new) + *M* (Arabic *mustaqbal*, future). Across this sweep's 3 searches and the ~150 EN/KO searches logged by the sibling sweeps, no Korean outlet, wiki or official page calls the project 네온시티/네온 시티; the consistent form is 네옴시티. The working title's "네온 시티" therefore reads to Korean viewers as a typo unless it is set up as a deliberate pun (e.g., show 네옴 → 네온 with a glitch, or use "거울 도시" instead). A dedicated search for "네온시티 사우디" could not be run (budget exhausted), so this is negative evidence.
